AUGUST 2026 — JULY 2027 · BAG · BUILD ACROSS GAMES

先证明共同基础，再扩展产品家族。

年度目标不是堆叠所有编辑器类型，而是交付可公开使用的 v0.8 Beta：稳定的 BAG IR、共用编辑基础、Unreal 正式支持、Godot 跨引擎验证，以及受控的 LLM Patch 工作流。

Prove the shared foundation before expanding the product family.

The goal is a usable v0.8 Beta with stable BAG IR, shared authoring infrastructure, production-grade Unreal support, cross-engine validation in Godot, and a controlled LLM Patch workflow.

Target: v0.8 Public BetaPrimary: Unreal EngineReference: GodotPreview: Unity

  • Monorepo and engineering rules
