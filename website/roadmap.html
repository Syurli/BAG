AUGUST 2026 — JULY 2027

一年内，从开放协议走向可用产品。

年度目标不是堆叠所有编辑器类型，而是交付可公开使用的 v0.8 Beta：战斗动作编辑闭环、稳定的 CrossLoom IR、Unreal 正式支持、Godot 跨引擎验证，以及受控的 LLM Patch 工作流。

From an open protocol to a usable product in one year.

The goal is not to accumulate every possible editor. It is to ship a usable v0.8 Beta with a complete combat-authoring loop, stable CrossLoom IR, production-grade Unreal support, cross-engine validation in Godot, and a controlled LLM Patch workflow.

Target: v0.8 Public BetaPrimary: Unreal EngineReference: GodotPreview: Unity

M5Public PreviewMonth 10

接入受控 LLM 编辑

Introduce controlled LLM authoring

AI 只能在用户授权的数据范围内提出结构化 Patch。

AI may only propose structured patches within the data scope authorized by the user.

Deliverables

  • 模型 Provider 抽象与 Schema 上下文
  • Patch 校验、可视化 Diff 与逐项批准
  • AI 修改来源、回滚与人工审核记录
  • 远程模型权限、脱敏与注入防护
  • Model-provider abstraction and schema context
  • Patch validation, visual diffs, and per-change approval
  • AI provenance, rollback, and human approval records
  • Remote-model permissions, redaction, and injection defenses

Exit criteria

  • 未经人工确认不能写入源文件
  • LLM 默认看不到最终资产与完整项目目录
  • 公开演示能明确说明 AI 边界
  • No source file is changed without human approval
  • LLMs do not see final assets or the whole project tree by default
  • The public demo clearly communicates AI boundaries
